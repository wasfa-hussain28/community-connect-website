About Us

We are dedicated to making communities safer and more responsive through technology.

Community Connect was created to bridge the gap between citizens and local authorities. Our platform provides a transparent, accessible way to report and track civic infrastructure issues.

Our Mission

Community First

Putting the needs of our community members at the forefront of everything we do.

Innovation

Using technology to solve real-world problems and improve civic engagement.

Transparency

Ensuring all civic issues are tracked and resolved with full visibility.

How It Works

  1.   User reports an issue via the portal
  2.   Issue is logged and assigned a unique ID
  3.   Local authorities are notified
  4.   Status updates to "Resolved" upon completion
